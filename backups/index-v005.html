5Bake the model into the Nano

Train a model first.
  1. Save myNanoModel.h in the sketch folder, next to nano33-ble-trainer.ino.
  2. In the Arduino IDE, open the sketch and press Upload. The file is detected automatically.
  3. Power the Nano from any USB supply, no computer needed. After a few seconds the RGB LED shows the class. Press Check Nano above to confirm it holds the baked model.
